Questions

Common questions

I build sites from scratch, and I move existing sites off page builders. Either way the site is hand-coded. I don't work inside page builders.

Websites start at $2,000. Bigger projects cost more, and you get the number before you commit to anything.

I don't have a website at all. Can you start from scratch?
Yes. It's the simplest kind of job: nothing to migrate and no old URLs to keep. The pages get planned around what you sell.
Do I own the site afterward?
Yes. You get the files, and the hosting and domain stay in your name.
I'm on Wix or Squarespace. Do I have to move?
To work with me, yes. I don't take on work inside page builders. Your URLs and wording come across with you, so the rankings can too.
Can you work on my existing site instead of rebuilding it?
If it's hand-coded or WordPress, yes. Audits, schema, metadata and speed work all happen in place. If it's on a builder, the audit still stands on its own, and the fixes come with the rebuild.
What do you need from me to start?
The URL. Access to Search Console and Analytics helps me set priorities, but a crawl needs neither, so the first look can start with the URL alone.
