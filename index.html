About

I care what a site looks like. A business can be doing everything right and still lose the job because its website looks thrown together, and that is a fixable problem.

So I build sites that look like someone cared, then do the search work underneath. When a tool I need does not exist, I build that too.

Builds start at $1,500

Bigger projects cost more, and you get the number before you commit to anything. New build or a rebuild off a page builder, it is hand-coded either way. I do not work inside a page builder.

I do not have a website at all. Can you start from scratch?

Yes, and it is the most straightforward version of this work. Nothing to migrate, no old URLs to preserve, and the structure gets built around what you actually sell instead of around whatever a template assumed.

Do I own the site afterward?

Yes. You get the files, and the hosting and domain stay in your name.

I am on Wix or Squarespace. Do I have to move?

To work with me, yes. I build hand-coded sites and I migrate sites onto them, but I do not take on work inside a page builder. Your URLs, wording and rankings come across with you.

Can you work on my existing site instead of rebuilding it?

If it is hand-coded or WordPress, yes. Audits, schema, metadata and speed work all happen in place. If it is on a builder, the audit still stands on its own, but the fixes come with the rebuild.

What do you need from me to start?

The URL. Access to Search Console and Analytics sharpens the priorities if you have them, but a crawl needs neither, so the first look can start with the URL alone.

Tell me about the site.

Building, rebuilding, or diagnosing. Send the URL and what you have tried.
