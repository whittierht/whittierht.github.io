01 Apps

Three apps for the agency

All three sit behind a company sign-in, so screenshots are what I can show.

Memphis

Project management

The agency's project management system, and the reason we stopped paying for Asana.

  • A REST API with scoped keys, so work can be created and timed from outside the app.
  • Messages go through Slack's API, so one sent here lands in the real workspace.
  • The old Asana board came across as an archive, with its columns and tags.

Tarmac

Staff time and payroll

Staff time and operations: an iPad kiosk, a phone portal and an admin panel. It started on Firebase and later moved to AWS Amplify.

  • PTO accrues per pay period. Holidays credit that day's scheduled hours.
  • The payroll export separates worked hours from paid hours.
  • Face clock-in at the kiosk is opt-in and runs on the iPad. No image leaves it.

Enterprise Mockups

Client review

The client review board. Reviewers pin comments to the exact spot, and the export names the file, the position and the element.

  • Frames can be PDFs, HTML files, images, live URLs or a zipped site.
  • Pins are saved as fractions of the frame. They hold at any zoom and stay put when the file is replaced.

There's a design editor inside it too, with live cursors for everyone in the file.

Deleting a board only archives it. A purge is what removes it for good.
