A business can be doing everything right and still lose the job because its website looks thrown together. That's fixable.

So I build sites that look like someone cared: real type, real spacing, the client's own photos instead of stock, and pages that hold up on a phone. Then I do the search work underneath. A good-looking site nobody can find is a brochure in a drawer.

When a tool I need doesn't exist, I build that too. Memphis exists because the agency outgrew Asana. The crawler exists because I wanted every page's title width, canonical and schema in one table.

Before this

From 2019 to 2021 I served a Spanish-speaking mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Tampa, Florida. Two years of knocking on doors in a language I was still learning taught me to explain things plainly.

My wife and I got married in 2022, and we live in Rexburg. The Tetons up top are close enough to visit on a Saturday.
