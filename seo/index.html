SEO

Enterprise and local SEO

I've been doing SEO for a year and a half. I'm the SEO specialist at a marketing agency in Rexburg, and my accounts run from a national payments processor to local service businesses.

01 Enterprise

A national payments processor

One of my accounts is a national payments processor and its co-branded programs. Their teams take the work live, so I don't name the company here.

  • Keyword architecture

    Every indexable page mapped to one target keyword, with a call on each URL: keep, rewrite, merge, redirect or retire.

  • Schema and metadata at scale

    A JSON-LD graph for every indexable page across the account, plus a rewritten title and description. All of it validated before handover.

  • Fixing an orphaned library

    A whole article section had no crawlable path into it. The controls that opened each category were buttons, and Googlebot doesn't click buttons.

  • One page type a week

    The content plan takes one page type each week. Its template gets built once, then rolls out to every page of that type.

  • Partner bank audits

    A bank's public web presence scored against a fixed framework, then a gap analysis and a roadmap. The crawl can run again for the next partner.

  • Support center rebuilds

    Pitched as working mockups instead of slides, built from their real content inside their real header and footer.

02 Local

Local businesses

On the local side I've worked with Bravo 6 Flight Academy, Outdoors for Youth, Madison Health and Nadia's Dog Nanny Services. I rebuilt three of those sites. Madison Health kept the one it had, and the search work happens there.

  • A page for each town

    Bravo 6 trains pilots in Rexburg and Pocatello, and each town has its own page. The schema on each one has that location's hours and coordinates, and names the airport.

  • Off Wix with every slug kept

    Outdoors for Youth had its content locked inside Wix iframes. I rebuilt it as 22 hand-coded pages and matched every slug to the old site, so the rankings carried over.

  • Off Divi

    Bravo 6 was a Divi site. Two Python scripts now build it as 135 static pages with clean URLs, and it moved onto the main domain in August.

  • Fixing a builder's schema

    GoDaddy's builder gave Nadia's site schema that pointed at a made-up domain. Now each of her 14 pages has one graph, and her seven services are listed with verified prices.

03 Audits

What I check on every site

My crawler checks status codes, whether each page can be indexed, canonicals, schema types and redirect chains. It runs against every sitemap the site lists.

Titles and descriptions get measured in pixels, because Google cuts them off by width.

Then I read the schema by hand. A graph can pass validation and still describe the wrong thing.
