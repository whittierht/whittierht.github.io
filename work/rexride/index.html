A ride board for Rexburg students

A buddy of mine wanted a ride board for students in Rexburg, so I'm building it. It isn't live yet, so for now it sits on a staging site. There's a demo with sample data you can click through.

Drivers post rides with open seats, and riders post requests. There's a map, a chat for every ride and a group chat for drivers.

You get a verified student badge with a .edu email. Your exact pickup pin stays private. Everyone else sees a three-mile area that's nudged off the real spot.

Pickup uses a QR code. After a ride you can say whether you'd ride with that person again. Admins and monitors handle reports.

The demo runs on sample rides and made-up people, and you don't need an account.
