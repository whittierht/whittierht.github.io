A builder caps what you can do for search

GoDaddy's builder wrote the schema, the sitemap and the titles, and it wouldn't let me fix any of them.

The business schema pointed at a made-up domain. The sitemap had junk URLs in it, and the titles came out double-encoded.

So the rebuild was about getting control back: 14 hand-coded pages from her own copy and prices, with one schema graph each.

The seven services are marked up as an offer catalog with verified prices. The certifications are real, and there's no review-star markup.
