Flight school software, from first call to checkride

Tarmac runs a flight school. I'm building it inside Bravo 6 Flight Academy, with the people who work their ramp in Rexburg and Pocatello. It isn't live yet, and other schools get in by invitation.

A booking on the schedule shows up on the dispatch board. Checking a plane out reads its Hobbs and tach. When it comes back, Tarmac logs the flight and emails the invoice.

Before a flight there's a risk check with 26 questions in five sections. Instructors grade each task and then the lesson. A recorded debrief turns into draft notes, and both of them sign the record.

Students get their own portal. They book and move flights, sign their lesson records and see how far along they are.

One record follows a student from their first phone call to their checkride. Records get archived, never deleted, and every staff account signs in with two steps.

The site has six free flight calculators, an E6B among them. Anyone can use them without signing up.
