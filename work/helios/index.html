A time clock and HR app for small teams

Helios turns an iPad by the door into a time clock. You find your name and tap a four-digit PIN to clock in. It's live, and new teams get in by invitation.

People can clock in from their phone too, with nothing to install. If someone forgets to clock out, Helios closes the shift at 11:59 pm and flags it for a look.

Face sign-in is optional, and each person decides for themselves. The iPad turns a face into 128 numbers and saves only those. No photo ever leaves the iPad.

PTO runs on the company's own policy, and the balance comes from the hours people actually work.

Helios doesn't hold pay rates. At the end of a pay period it checks the hours, flags anything that needs fixing and hands payroll one CSV file.

The HR side keeps the team directory and an org chart that redraws itself when someone gets a new manager. There are checklists for a first day and a last one, and documents get signed with a typed name.
