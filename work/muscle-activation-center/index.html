Notes during the appointment

Right now they take notes on paper during appointments. This app replaces the paper.

A visit has five steps: check-in, assess, muscle tests, reassess and plan. On the body map, one tap marks a spot mild, two is moderate and three is severe. A fourth tap clears it.

Muscles that tested weak last visit come up on their own. The last visit stays next to the current one the whole time.

The plan step sets the home exercises and the next visit. The exercises print as a sheet the client takes home.

Google Calendar appointments are built and switched off until the app is HIPAA compliant. Every client in these screenshots is made up.
