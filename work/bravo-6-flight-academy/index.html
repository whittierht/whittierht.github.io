From WordPress to 165 pages

Their old site was built with Divi on WordPress. Now it's 165 pages that my Python scripts build, and it just got a big upgrade.

The newest part is the flight training cost calculator. You drag sliders for flight, ground and simulator hours, and the estimate changes as you go. One switch jumps between the FAA minimum of 40 hours and the 60 hours the school lists for most students.

Is It Flyable Today checks the live airport weather against the school's own minimums. The training area map draws the practice areas over an FAA sectional chart.

The fleet page lists seven airplanes and a Redbird simulator with their hourly rates. Every training program has its own page now.

The site launched at 135 pages. Every page sits in its own folder, so clean URLs work on plain shared hosting.

Rexburg and Pocatello each have their own page, with schema for that location. There's more about those on the SEO page.
