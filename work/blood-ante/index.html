How it plays

I make a game on the side. It's a pixel-art card game that runs in the browser, on a phone or a computer.

Ten floors an ante. Find the doors. Build the deck from nothing.

Your weapons fire on their own. You pick up playing cards along the way and play them as hands, and damage is might times mult.

There are four knights to pick from, difficulty from Easy to Extremely Hard, and leaderboards. Sign-in is optional, and every old version is still there to play.
