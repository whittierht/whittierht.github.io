Off Wix, rankings intact

On Wix, the camp's best content was locked inside iframes. Search engines saw the frame and not the text.

Now it's 22 hand-coded pages, and every slug matches the old site, so the rankings carried over.

Registration is a seven-step form. It saves answers so a parent can finish later, and it totals rentals as they're added.

The session tiles come from the camp's Salesforce inventory. The waitlist only shows up once a week has sold out.
