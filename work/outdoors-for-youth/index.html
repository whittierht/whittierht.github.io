A camp site with paid registration

The big news is registration. It's a custom form I built, it takes payments, and it's finally live.

Registration for 2027 camp is seven steps, and you pay on the last one by card or bank account. A parent can sign up several campers and pay once.

Card numbers get typed inside the card processor's own frame. Only a token comes back, so the numbers never touch the site's server.

The form saves your progress on your device, so you can step away and finish later. Rentals and camp apparel are in the form too. Promo codes work, and a camper on the waitlist isn't charged.

The session tiles come from the camp's Salesforce inventory. Click one and the form opens with that week already picked.

Before all this, the site was on Wix, and the camp's best content was stuck inside iframes. I rebuilt it as 22 hand-coded pages and kept every slug, so the rankings carried over.
