Eight releases on one page

A website for Loafa, an alt rock band from Provo. It's one HTML file with no libraries.

Every song has a 30-second preview. All eight releases, from 2022 to Unit 210, sit on a timeline that scrolls sideways.

Unit 210 came out October 1, 2026, and it gets its own yellow band near the top of the page.

Press from Provo Music Magazine and City Weekly is laid out as torn clippings. Past shows at Velour and other venues are under Live. The videos come from the band's Instagram and YouTube, and the last section is for booking.
